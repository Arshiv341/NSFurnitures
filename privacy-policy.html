Privacy Policy

Learn how we protect and manage the information you submit on our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you request an estimate using our contact form, we collect the personal details you submit (specifically your Name, Phone Number, Email address, and message details). This information is solely used to process and coordinate your furniture repair quote request.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to communicate with you about your quote request, coordinate furniture pickup and delivery timelines, and verify that the restoration matches your requirements. We do not use this data for marketing newsletters or cold calls.

3. Data Protection

We take reasonable technical precautions to safeguard the personal details you submit on our site. Your data is stored securely and is only accessible by verified workshop personnel processing your orders.

4. Third-Party Sharing

NS Furniture does not sell, lease, or share your contact details or personal information with third-party advertising companies. Your details are shared only when necessary to process payments or coordinate courier deliveries.

5. Contact Queries

If you have questions about your personal data records or would like us to remove your contact logs from our systems, please reach out to us directly at Furniturens2@gmail.com.
