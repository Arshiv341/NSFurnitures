Our Philosophy

Craftsmanship & Care

Restoring structural strength and visual beauty to residential and commercial furniture.

Who We Are

Furniture Restoration Specialists

NS Furniture is a dedicated local restoration and carpentry workshop. We focus on rebuilding sagging couches, polishing worn wooden frames, and customizing upholstery to transform aged furniture into premium home highlights.

Rather than using prefabricated templates, every piece is inspected individually. We select durable hardwoods, calculate web load alignments, and install high-density cushioning to extend the comfort and structural durability of your furniture.

Quality Guidelines

Our Material Standards

We never compromise on the inner structural components of your furniture.

Timber Selection

We use seasoned hardwoods (such as Teak, Sal, or Marandi) to replace fractured frame structural members. This prevents wobbly joints and ensures long-term resistance.

High-Density Padding

We install 32-density to 40-density resilient foams, combined with heavy-duty elastic webbing bands. This prevents future sagging and provides comfortable support.

Premium Fabrics

We offer stain-resistant velvet, high-tensile woven cottons, and premium faux leatherette options from top manufacturers. Designed to withstand wear and maintain texture.

Quality Checks

Workshop Inspection Checklist

Every restored piece of furniture undergoes a multi-point inspection before leaving our workshop:

  • Frame alignment and wobble check
  • Upholstery alignment and seam pattern testing
  • Button tufting tension and spacing uniformity
  • Multi-layer wood polish grain reveal and seal check
